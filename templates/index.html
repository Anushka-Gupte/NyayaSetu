सुरक्षा · SECURITY

Security & Compliance Architecture

Engineered to satisfy stringent legal evidentiary requirements and high-concurrency non-functional specifications.

SHA-256 Tamper-Evidence

Every legal document and electronic exhibit is fingerprinted with cryptographic SHA-256 hashing upon upload. Any subsequent amendment creates a fresh version with full provenance.

Cryptographic Virtual Rooms

Virtual hearings enforce role-gated access with cryptographically randomized passcodes and room tokens. Unauthorized parties are prevented from entering proceedings.

Immutable Security Audit Trail

Every authentication event, case status modification, schedule override, and document access is immutably logged with actor identity, timestamp, and client network parameters.
